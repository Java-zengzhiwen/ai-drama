# Selected Direction

## Direction

**Asset Detail Review Page**

## Why This Direction

The first generated M2 directions were structurally correct but too data-management heavy. The selected direction makes asset quality judgment visual and direct.

This direction works because:

- asset review depends on seeing identity, costume, scene layout, and prop clarity;
- a dedicated detail page gives the image enough space;
- version comparison supports choosing the current adopted asset;
- the right inspector preserves M1's decision and gate language;
- related requirements keep the asset tied to shots and Shot Prompt readiness;
- the flow does not require a complex DAM or image editor.

## Locked Decisions

- Keep asset detail nested under the chapter workspace.
- Keep the image preview large and dominant.
- Keep version history visible on the detail page.
- Keep asset decisions in the right inspector.
- Keep `Agnes 生成` and `结果与重跑` locked in M2.
- Do not add professional image editing controls.
- Do not replace M1 table and inspector language with a new visual system.
