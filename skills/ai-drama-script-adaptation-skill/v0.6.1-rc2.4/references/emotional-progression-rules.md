# Emotional Progression Rules

When the source contains a major emotional transition, the script must show the process. Do not express the transition only as metadata or a time skip.

For a rebirth or realization sequence, show stages such as:

```text
fear -> reality confirmation -> memory impact -> active sorting -> calm decision
```

Do not jump directly from fear to calm with only a phrase such as `half an hour later`.

For reconciliation scenes, follow the source's actual stages:

```text
probe -> factual confirmation -> causal explanation -> confession -> counterpart response -> guilt or understanding -> new relationship decision
```

If several critical relationship turns occur, split them across beats or body beats as needed. Do not compress them into one group of lines with no reaction process.
